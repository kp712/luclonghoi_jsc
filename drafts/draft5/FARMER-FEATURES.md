# Farmer features for the Draft 5 home page

Short research notes (14 Nov 2026) on what coastal Mekong farmers (Sóc Trăng, Bạc Liêu, Bến Tre, Trà Vinh) need from a salt and crop-planning tool. Quotes are short and verbatim from each page. The web pages were read as data, not instructions.

| # | Feature | Why farmers need it | Source and quote | Built? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **When salt usually arrives** (countdown plus a 12-month salt strip per area) | Farmers say they lack timely salt information to judge their water sources. Knowing the month salt usually arrives tells them how long they have to prepare. | Mekong Salt Lab (Trà Vinh), FreshPlaza, 11 Dec 2023, https://www.freshplaza.com/article/9584517/innovative-and-practical-support-to-farmers-adapting-to-salinity/ : "Farmers lack access to timely and useful (salinity) data" | **Yes**: `#hmNow` (salt months from `RX.DATA.sites`, labelled as an estimate from station reports, not satellite) |
| 2 | **Store fresh water before the dry season** | Sóc Trăng advises keeping fresh water for production in case salt comes in. | Nông nghiệp và Môi trường, 23 Feb 2024, https://nongnghiepmoitruong.vn/xam-nhap-man-o-soc-trang-dien-bien-phuc-tap-trong-nhung-ngay-toi-d376951.html : "tích trữ nguồn nước ngọt phục vụ sản xuất trong khoảng 15 ngày" | **Yes**: tip 1 in `#hmPrep` |
| 3 | **Measure salt before pumping water in** (rice needs under 1‰) | Water is checked before every pump-in. The Sóc Trăng summer–autumn advice sets the 1‰ limit before sowing. | Same article: "kiểm tra nguồn nước trước khi lấy nước vào bơm, tưới"; Nhân Dân, 20 May 2024, https://nhandan.vn/chu-dong-canh-tac-vu-lua-he-thu-hieu-qua-post810147.html : "Tiến hành đo độ mặn dưới 1‰" | **Yes**: tip 2 in `#hmPrep` |
| 4 | **Follow the local sowing calendar (lịch xuống giống)**: sow only when fresh water is steady | Officials set sowing windows and advise against sowing before fresh water is reliable. | Nhân Dân, 20 May 2024 (same URL): "không nên gieo sạ nếu chưa có nguồn nước ngọt ổn định"; Open Development Mekong, 18 Apr 2020, https://vietnam.opendevelopmentmekong.net/news/rice-farmers-told-to-follow-sowing-schedules-amid-saline-intrusion-drought/ : "schedules for sowing the crop in the delta between March and June" | **Yes**: tip 3 in `#hmPrep`; the 12-month plan itself is the app's main flow |
| 5 | **Salt bulletins on Zalo, radio and loudspeakers** | Sóc Trăng posts salt readings through mass media and Zalo groups. | 6 | **Dry-season rain outlook** (rain now compared with the salt month) | When rain stops, rivers carry less fresh water and salt moves inland. | 7 | **El Niño warning** | El Niño years bring earlier, stronger salt. Farmers need to hear it early. | NOAA outlook already in `DATA.outlook`; the app's station notes give higher El Niño peaks (for example 17.6‰ against 15.8‰ at Khánh Hòa) | **Yes**: kept as `#hmAlert`, with one line of advice added |
| 8 | **Salt-tolerant rice varieties** (OM5451, ST24) | Coastal pilots use salt-tolerant rice. ## What the home page now shows
- `#hmArt`: a calm inline-SVG picture (sun, sea, paddy plots, river) in theme colours.
- Title, a one-line lead, **Start**, saved fields, then "How it works" as 3 pictures (`#hmSteps`).
- `#hmAlert`: NOAA El Niño alert plus one line of advice.
- `#hmNow`: "This month on the coast". It shows how long until salt usually comes and where it comes first, the rain this month against the salt month (NASA GPM IMERG), and one tappable row per area with its status and a 12-month salt strip. Tapping a row closes home and opens that area. The salt months are labelled as an estimate from station reports, not satellite.
- `#hmPrep`: "Before the salt comes". Four tickable tips, each naming its source. Ticks are stored in this browser only (`localStorage` key `d5-prep`).
- `#hmData`: "Where the numbers come from": GPM IMERG, POWER, MODIS, and station reports for salt.
