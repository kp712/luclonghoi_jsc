How the app scores a farming plan: the basics

A short guide for 9709 A Level Mathematics students who know Pure 1. Real numbers from the Vĩnh Châu area of the Mekong Delta, Vietnam.

1. What the app does

Salty seawater enters the Mekong rivers in the dry season (January–May). The app compares 5 farming plans and gives each one 4 scores from 1 to 5: Water, Weather & salt, Soil, Income. A plan is 12 letters, one per month:

PlanJFMAMJJASOND
3 rice cropsRR-RRRRRRRRR
2 rice cropsRR-RRRR---RR
Rice–shrimpSSSSSS-RRRR-

R = rice, S = shrimp (lives in salty water), - = rest.

Key idea. Every score compares a plan with 3 rice crops (what most farmers do now). So 3 rice crops always get Water 1 and Income 3.

2. Two tools from Pure 1

A straight line through two points (1.3 Coordinate geometry). Gradient $m=\dfrac{y_2-y_1}{x_2-x_1}$, then $y=mx+c$.

A "clamped" function (1.2 Functions). If a score comes out below 1 it becomes 1; above 5 it becomes 5. The graph is a line with flat ends. Its range is $1\le y\le5$.

3. How much canal water a month needs

$$\text{water needed}=\text{crop use}+\text{seepage}-\text{useful rain}\qquad(\text{never below }0)$$
  • Crop use $=K\times E$: $E$ is how much water the air takes from grass that month (from NASA weather data); $K$ says how thirsty the crop is (rice: 1.05 at the start, 1.20 in the middle, 0.75 at the end).
  • Seepage: a flooded rice field loses 4 mm a day into the ground.
  • Useful rain is a piecewise function of the month's rain $P$ (mm):
$$P_{\text{eff}}=\begin{cases}0.8P-25 & P>75\\ 0.6P-10 & P\le75\ \ (\text{but not below }0)\end{cases}$$
Check the join. At $P=75$: $0.8(75)-25=35$ and $0.6(75)-10=35$. Same value, so the graph has no jump.

Example, rice in January: $E=122$, $P=27$, 31 days.
Useful rain $=0.6(27)-10=6.2$. Water needed $=1.20(122)+4(31)-6.2=146.4+124-6.2\approx\mathbf{264}$ mm.

Adding all 12 months gives the year: 1925 mm for 3 rice crops, 1733 mm for 2 rice crops (extra soaking and flooding at the start of each rice crop are included).

4. Water score: a straight line

Let $s$ = fraction of water saved compared with 3 rice crops: $s=1-\dfrac{W}{1925}$.

We want $s=0\to$ score 1 and $s=1\to$ score 5. Line through $(0,1)$ and $(1,5)$: gradient $\frac{5-1}{1-0}=4$, so

$$\text{Water}=1+4s$$

2 rice crops: $s=1-\frac{1733}{1925}=0.0997$, Water $=1+4(0.0997)\approx\mathbf{1.4}$.
Rice–shrimp: $W=372$, $s=0.807$, Water $\approx\mathbf{4.2}$.

5. Weather & salt score: a weighted average

Three risks, each between 0 (safe) and 1 (worst): salt (share of salty months with a crop in the field), heat (days of 35 °C or more) and canal (canal water needed while canals are salty). Each risk $r$ becomes a part score with the line through $(0,5)$ and $(1,1)$:

$$\text{part}=5-4r,\qquad \text{Weather \& salt}=0.6\times\text{salt part}+0.2\times\text{heat part}+0.2\times\text{canal part}$$

The weights add up to 1, so the answer stays between 1 and 5.

3 rice crops: crops are in the field in 4 of the 5 salty months, so $r=0.8$ and salt part $=5-3.2=1.8$. No hot days: heat part 5. It uses all the canal water of 3 rice crops: canal part 1.
Score $=0.6(1.8)+0.2(5)+0.2(1)=1.08+1+0.2\approx\mathbf{2.3}$.

Rice–shrimp: shrimp fill January–May, so every risk is 0 and the score is $\mathbf{5.0}$.

6. Soil score: counting months

$$\text{Soil}=1+(\text{rest points})+(\text{non-rice points})+(\text{bean points})$$

Each kind of month gives $\tfrac12$ point per month, up to 1 point (2 months is enough). Shrimp leaves salt in the soil: $-0.5$.

3 rice crops: 1 rest month, $1+0.5=\mathbf{1.5}$. 2 rice crops: 4 rest months, $1+1=\mathbf{2.0}$.

7. Income score: doubling and halving

$P$ = a plan's yearly profit, worked out from the middle value (median) of published figures. 3 rice crops: $40+25.4+30=95.4$ million đồng per hectare. 2 rice crops: $40+25.4=65.4$.

We want: same profit as 3 rice crops $\to$ 3, double $\to$ 5, half $\to$ 1. Each doubling adds 2 points. The tool that counts doublings is $\log_2$ ("how many times do I double 1 to get $x$?"; you meet it properly in Pure 2/3):

$$\text{Income}=3+2\log_2\!\Big(\frac{P}{95.4}\Big)$$

2 rice crops: $65.4/95.4=0.686$, about 0.55 of a halving, so the score is about $3-2(0.545)=\mathbf{1.9}$.

9. Quick questions

  1. Find the useful rain when $P=100$ mm.
  2. A plan saves half the water. What is its Water score?
  3. Find the Weather & salt score if the parts are salt 4.2, heat 5, canal 3.
  4. Which water saving $s$ gives a Water score of 3?
  5. A plan earns 4 times as much as 3 rice crops. The formula gives $3+2(2)=7$. What score does the app show, and why?

Answers: 1. $0.8(100)-25=55$ mm. 2. $1+4(0.5)=3$. 3. $2.52+1+0.6=4.12\to4.1$. 4. $1+4s=3\Rightarrow s=0.5$. 5. Score 5: the result is clamped because the range is $1\le\text{score}\le5$.
